How to play
  • Move with the arrow keys or W A S D. Walk into a monster to hit it.
  • Space swings your weapon (or fires your bow or staff) in the way you are facing.
  • 1 2 3 use your three class abilities. Q drinks a healing potion.
  • E is your do-something key: stairs, shopkeepers, and helping a friend up.
  • I opens your pack. Click an item to equip or drink it.
  • If you fall you are down, not dead. A friend can stand over you and hold E.
  • Stand on the stairs and press E to call everyone down to the next level.

Going down: stand on the staircase and press E. Everyone on the level travels together after a short countdown, so nobody gets left behind.

If you fall: you are down, not dead. A friend standing next to you can hold E to bring you back. If the whole party falls, the temple pulls you home to Aldershade and takes a tenth of your gold for the trouble.

Town: Doran sells weapons and armour, Wren sells potions and scrolls, and Sister Halli will heal you for coin. The keep gate is at the top of the road.
